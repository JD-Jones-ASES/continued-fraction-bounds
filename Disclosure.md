# Authorship and automated assistance

JD Jones is the human author and responsible maintainer of this project.
OpenAI Codex agents using GPT-6 developed mathematical arguments, Lean
proofs, documentation, and verification tooling under his direction.
GPT-6 Pro supplied a mathematical review, proposed the maximum inverse
estimate and optimal window cutoffs, and supplied an independent exact
arithmetic implementation. Codex agents adapted that implementation and
formalized and checked the extensions. Gemini-Argon contributed preliminary
mathematical discussion. These systems
are credited as automated assistance, not as human authors or maintainers.

Lean tactics construct proof terms for kernel checking. No finite numerical
sample, external language-model answer, or unproved mathematical assertion
serves as a proof oracle. The exact Python replays are additional finite
checks and are not proofs of unrestricted quantified statements.

Mechanical verification, mathematical review, worldwide priority, and
registry registration are separate matters. No independent human
specialist peer review or worldwide-priority determination is claimed.
The formal contract and exact-commit verification evidence are described
in [Verification.md](Verification.md).

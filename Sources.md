# Mathematical sources and scope

The ordinary arbitrary-digit Borel minimum threshold and Tong maximum
threshold are classical. Natural-extension coordinates and geometric
optimization of approximation coefficients are also established methods.
The results studied here refine those bounds using exact terminated
runs and give optimal global linear inverse estimates for both extrema. The accompanying
proofs are the mathematical account of this project, not a separate
earlier source. No worldwide-priority claim is made.

Savita S. Adhin, Ayreena Bakhtawar and Cor Kraaikamp,
[*Sharpening Borel's result in Diophantine approximation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.04421v1),
arXiv:2607.04421v1, 5 July 2026, is the immediate mathematical motivation.
The [versioned full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.04421v1) refines the
minimum using runs of digit $1$. Equations (15)–(16) recall the classical
arbitrary-digit minimum and maximum thresholds. Section 5 explicitly
suggests the arbitrary-digit refinement and the corresponding refinement
of Tong's maximum bound. This suggestion and the geometric approach are
credited to those authors. The present account treats all integer central
digits $a\ge2$, both objectives, exact departure types, actual
continued-fraction realization, and both inverse-stability coefficients and optimal digit-window cutoffs.

This literature account identifies the immediate continuation question and
the known mathematical inputs. Several originals remain unread. Failure
to find a matching formula is not evidence of absence. The package does
not claim to classify a Markov or Lagrange spectrum or to settle priority.

The intended audience is researchers in continued fractions and
Diophantine approximation interested in sharp conditional approximation
constants, their extremizers, and quantitative rigidity. The full
continued-fraction meaning requires both the optimization theorem and
the ordinary-convergent realization argument; a real-coordinate identity
alone does not establish sharpness over actual irrational inputs.
